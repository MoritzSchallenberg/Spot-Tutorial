# About ALeRT and Spot

## Overview

The background needed to understand why this tutorial exists and what
it documents: the ALeRT team, the RoboCup Rescue League the team
competes in, and the Spot platform the rest of this site is built
around.

## Purpose

This section is historical and contextual, not procedural. It explains
where the team and the platform come from before the rest of the site
covers how to operate and understand the system itself.

## System context

Nothing here is a live system claim — see
[Safety and Prerequisites](../safety/index.md),
[Operating Spot](../operating/index.md) and
[System Architecture](../architecture/index.md) for the current,
code-grounded technical documentation.
