# Historical Gallery

## Overview

Photographs and competition-arena images from the former ALeRT
tutorial, documenting past RoboCup Rescue League participation and the
Spot platform.

:::{admonition} Historical snapshot
:class: note

These images date from the former ALeRT tutorial and past competition
years. They are not evidence of the team's or the platform's current
state.
:::

## Provenance

Every image below is migrated unchanged (no recompression, no
upscaling) from the former ALeRT tutorial's source material, with its
origin, checksum comparison, and alt text tracked internally in
`maintainers/historical-asset-migration.md`. Images that showed
credentials, network information, or other sensitive content in the
source material were excluded rather than migrated — see
`maintainers/spot-security-audit.md` (internal).

## Competition and team

**Award ceremony** — the ALeRT team at a RoboCup Rescue League award
ceremony, with the competition trophy.

**Spot on gravel** — Spot seated on a gravel/rubble-like surface,
illustrating the kind of terrain the competition's maneuvering and
mobility challenge tests.

**RRL Eindhoven group photo** — a RoboCup Rescue League group/event
photograph, referenced alongside the tutorial's account of the
league's origin.

**Rescue league teams** — teams competing in the RoboCup Rescue League.

## Arena and terrain

**Arena** (three variants) — the competition arena used for the
maneuvering, mobility and exploration challenges across different
tutorial revisions.

**RRL arena** — the Rescue League competition arena referenced in the
team's course introduction material.

**Terrain** (four variants) — terrain types used in the "updated tasks
for smaller teams" ruleset described in [RoboCup Rescue](robocup-rescue.md).

**Maneuvering** — an obstacle/surface setup from the maneuvering and
mobility challenge.

## Dexterity and exploration

**Dexterity board** — the dexterity test board used in the dexterity
challenge.

**Door** — a door-opening/turning task from the dexterity challenge.

**Emergency** — imagery associated with the "sign of life" / emergency
scenario aspect of the exploration challenge.

**Exploration** (four variants) — exploration-challenge scenes: 2D/3D
mapping, object detection, and hole/terrain avoidance.

**Objective** (two variants) — objectives to detect, from the "updated
tasks for smaller teams" ruleset.

**Readiness** — the readiness-test setup (sensor and dexterity
capability checks).

**Tests** — the new readiness tests introduced in the "updated tasks
for smaller teams" ruleset.

## Verification

`Historical procedure` — every image on this page documents a past
state of the competition or the team, not current equipment,
personnel, or rules.
